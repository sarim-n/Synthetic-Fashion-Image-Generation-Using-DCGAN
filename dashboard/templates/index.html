Interactive step-by-step visual representation of the Deep Convolutional GAN training process. Walk through the complete adversarial pipeline from random latent noise to Generator synthesis, Discriminator evaluation, BCE loss computation, and parameter updates.

Architecture Context: Each generated image starts from a random 100-dimensional latent vector z ~ N(0, I). Latent vector z does not learn; fresh noise is sampled for every batch.
